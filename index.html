A data-driven view of customer churn patterns, designed to help business teams identify retention risks and choose practical follow-up actions.

About the Data

This analysis uses customer-level telecom service records to explore which customer groups leave more often. This context explains the data behind the dashboard before you explore the results.

Source · IBM Telco Customer Churn
Dataset overview
Telecom customer behaviour
Each row represents a customer and contains service, account, contract, billing and churn information. The outcome column is Churn: Yes or No.
Fields explored
Contract · Tenure · Monthly charges
These three attributes are used to compare group-level churn rates. They are selected for an accessible, business-focused dashboard.

Data preparation & limitations
The analysis script removes duplicate customer IDs, converts tenure and monthly charges to numeric values, and excludes records missing required analysis fields. Tenure groups are New (0–12 months), Existing (13–36 months), and Long-term (37+ months); bill groups are Low (under $35), Medium ($35 to under $70), and High ($70 or more). Group churn rates describe historical associations, not causation or individual customer predictions. The recommendations are rule-based suggestions and should be tested against measured retention outcomes.
